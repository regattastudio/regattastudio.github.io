Why sailors love it

Everything your race committee needs

Regatta Studio is a free, cloud-based app for scoring informal one-design racing from your phone or tablet. Perfect for club racing and virtual sailing.

On-the-water scoring

Record finishes as boats cross the line. Results are calculated almost instantly.

Simple entry management

Competitors register themselves from their own devices. No more transcribing sign-up sheets.

Push notifications

Post notices and course changes and every competitor gets pinged right away.

Everything in the cloud

Multiple scorers can enter data at the same time, and everyone always sees the latest.

Live results

Results before you're back at the dock

Scores are captured in real time and standings update as soon as each race is scored. Competitors can check results from the water, the parking lot, or the bar.

  • Discards, penalties and codes like OCS, DSQ and UFD
  • Multiple classes and multi-day series
  • Shareable results page for your club website
Works offline

Spotty signal? No problem.

Data is cached on your device during network interruptions and syncs back to the cloud automatically when you're back in range.

  • Set up an event in under a minute
  • Share scoring duties with your committee
  • Free to download and use
